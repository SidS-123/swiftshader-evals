## Analysis

Measured 2026-10-01, metric `ssvk-1.0`, image `sha256:82a05e1f...`, corpus at
`a1a8ee9`, on AC power with no standby during any graded run (Windows
Kernel-Power log checked against every run's window; the final sweep ran
18:10-18:13 and, after a pause on battery, 20:49-21:29, one control at a time;
the generated provenance line above shows only the last control's block).
Predictions were committed before any control ran (`33b80a9`); the comparison
is `tools/controls_vs_predictions.py`. **32 of 38 rows (19 controls x 2 splits)
hit their committed band; the 6 misses are three controls on both splits, all
traced to the predictor, none to the metric.** Public and hidden agree within
0.02 on every control.

**The grader accepts its definer.** `reference`: 1.000 on every category,
both splits, full success. Its timed runs measure 0.82-1.05x the cached
reference times, which is this host's run-to-run spread (geometry is the
noisiest case).

**The floor.** `stub` (the task's starter): 0.013 public, 0.007 hidden; only
the loader's instance version matches. `init_only` 0.12 and
`success_everywhere` 0.23: reporting SwiftShader's profile and returning the
right call results is worth something on procedural checks, nothing on replay
or performance. These are the null band a model's early attempts will sit in.

**Memorisation is defeated by the hidden split.** `hardcode_public` (the
oracle on a byte-identical public case file, `init_only` otherwise) scores
1.000 with full success on public and 0.124 on hidden, the `init_only` level.
A candidate can read the case file (`/case.json`) from inside its process; the
hidden split is what makes that useless.

**Visible output errors are caught where they occur and only there.**
`gain_x2` (colour x2) and `half_pixel` (+0.5 px viewport) zero every image
family and leave the compute and buffer families at 1.0: replay 0.49-0.56,
because replay is the mean over families. `aux_garbage` zeroes every snapshot
that holds a depth or stencil item. `srgb_mishandled` only alters readbacks of
sRGB images, which few cases snapshot (replay 0.97-0.98). `nearest_filter`
zeroes the cases that sample with linear filters, except `textureGather` and
anisotropic sampling, which ignore the min/mag filter (replay 0.96). None of
them reaches full success.

**Harmless differences are free.** `round_trunc` (about half of 8-bit codes
one LSB low) scores exactly 1.000: the free 1 LSB applies before the bias
term, by design (restated before measuring). The perturbed-reference control
(`python -m evalbase.grader.perturbed`, offline from the caches) confirms the
tolerance: `vtxjitter` 0.995 public / 0.989 hidden, `texcoord_ulp` 1.000, the
ThreadCount-1 run 1.000; every case below 0.7 under jitter has its threshold
at the `t_hi` cap (one public, three hidden `raster_tri` clip cases), the
exemption the plan states.

**Sequences catch a candidate one step behind.** `stale_frame` (each image
readback returns the previous one) scores 0.72-0.73 replay. It first measured
0.74-0.75 because the `compute_image` cases repeated their output across
dispatches; they were fixed so the second snapshot differs (Stage 8 findings).

**Procedural checks bite, and the bar is a mean.** `wrong_errors`
(VK_SUCCESS instead of FORMAT_NOT_SUPPORTED, INCOMPLETE, TIMEOUT, NOT_READY)
loses 12-14 % of procedural checks and full success. `wrong_limits` (two wrong
limits, one wrong format bit) loses 3 % and *keeps* full success: the
procedural bar is a mean of 0.95 (kept, see DESIGN.md).

**Hostile candidates do not break the grader.** `crash` (SIGSEGV on the
second submit) and `malformed` (2^60-byte memory requirements) produce scores
(0.32, 0.14), no grader exception; snapshots taken before the crash still
count.

**Other drivers.** `reference_subzero`: replay 0.996-0.999, procedural 0.98
(`deviceName` differs, and the properties key is graded whole), no full
success (`formats_copy_blit` blits differ at Subzero's float precision).
`lavapipe` (Mesa): replay 0.84 (subgroup size 8, compressed decoding, blits,
MSAA, depth differ), procedural 0.62-0.63 (it fails every device-identity key:
properties, features, extensions, memory, queue families, versions, and passes
the sync, query and most call-result checks), performance 0.5 (two timed runs
fail fidelity). A correct but different driver lands around 0.74 overall.

**Performance (D5).** Timed-run ratios to the reference on AC: `one_thread`
2.05-3.60, `reference_subzero` 1.02-4.69, `lavapipe` 0.47-0.73 where its
output matches. Every JIT-class driver measured is far inside the 8x gate and
scores ~1.0 on performance. D5 is decided in DESIGN.md: keep 16 / 8.

**Misses, restated after measurement (marked, next to the originals in DESIGN.md).**
`crash` procedural 0.67-0.68 vs 0.50-0.51: the predictor failed the
VkResult-stream check on cases where the crash never fires (fewer than two
submits). `lavapipe` procedural 0.62-0.63 vs 0.23: the predictor assumed every
check after the device fails; only device-identity checks do. `nearest_filter`
performance 1.0 vs 0.75: `perf_texture` samples anisotropically, and the
reference's anisotropic path ignores the min/mag filter.

**What the controls found wrong with the eval (fixed before these numbers).**
Four advanced-blend cases never created their device on the reference
(found by `crash`); one case snapshotted memory it never wrote (found by
`hardcode_public`), which led to a new gate that replays every case with
poisoned allocations; the `compute_image` cases could not detect stale output
(found by `stale_frame`). Details: `docs/internal/stage8_findings.md`.
